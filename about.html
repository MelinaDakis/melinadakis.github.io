About me

I've spent eight years shaping brand voice across consumer retail, a major auction house, and entertainment, writing for how people actually scan, feel, and act, not just what looks good on a content calendar. That work taught me that the words on a screen are never just words, they're the interface between a brand and the person trying to get something done.

Right now I'm formalizing that instinct into a discipline. I'm enrolled in the Interaction Design Foundation's UX Writer & Content Designer career program, building the structural thinking behind good writing: object models, feature breakdowns, conceptual models, and design maps. I've also spent time testing AI tools against real domain expertise, seeing exactly where they hold up and where they still need a human who has actually lived the problem.

I'm looking for a UX writing or content design role where I can bring both halves of that experience: a writer's instinct for voice, and a newly sharpened eye for the structure underneath it.
